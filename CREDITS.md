# Sources and adaptation record

## Course

Based on Yongfu Li’s [Placement Lesson 06 — analytical / density-aware place](https://yongfu-li.github.io/eda-learning/courses/learn_placement/labs/analytical-place/index.html), [original browser lab](https://yongfu-li.github.io/eda-learning/tools/analytical-place/index.html), and [eda-learning repository](https://github.com/yongfu-li/eda-learning).

The research snapshot is fixed to revision `003bcd49e3794e6cfd2680181cdbd347d673ad07`. Source paths include `courses/learn_placement/module02-05-analytical-place/` and `platform/assets/placement-core.js`. Lesson 06 uses module directory number 02-05; they refer to the same course module. Archived sources, hashes and numerical checks are retained in the parent project’s `research/` folder.

The tiny netlist and reference update rules follow that source. The website’s domain implementation has been checked against the archived implementation at five density weights, including all fixed-anchor coordinates. The visual narration, semantic timing, components, companion comments and practice activities are new adaptations. The capacity-2 layouts are a separately labelled designed example.

The upstream course README states an intention to align its materials with CC BY 4.0 and says to add a LICENSE when publishing. The course LICENSE file and GitHub’s repository-license endpoint did not supply a licence document when checked. This record preserves attribution without treating that intention as a verified licence grant. No upstream videos, audio recordings or slide images are redistributed in this website.

## Character

The cartoon head is adapted from [Einstein by j4p4n](https://openclipart.org/detail/302755/einstein), Openclipart, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Original artwork and provenance are included in `assets/`. The new articulated body, hand gestures, fixed closed smile and small white moustache are editable SVG/code adaptations. This illustrated educational companion does not imply Einstein’s participation or voice.

Arm movement uses an independently written two-bone 2D inverse-kinematics solver, informed by the hand-target and elbow-direction concepts described in the [Blender manual](https://docs.blender.org/manual/id/5.1/animation/constraints/tracking/ik_solver.html). The animation runs directly in JavaScript; Blender is not a runtime dependency.

## Narration, subtitles and music

Narration uses the named synthetic service voices Thomas, Guy, Prabhat and Yunxi through `edge_tts`, under the user’s explicit authorisation to send lesson text to the online voice service. Voice availability and configuration are recorded in `content/voice-provenance.json`. These are service voice identities, not clones of a public figure or endorsements by a broadcaster.

The low-volume music is an original synthesised pluck motif and sustained harmonic bed, carried forward from the approved bright sample. It is generated by `scripts/make_course_audio.py`; no third-party music recording or paid music asset is included. Narration and music remain separately editable in `production/`.

Captions are supplied in English, Chinese, French, Spanish, German, Japanese and Korean. Translations preserve the semantic cue, quantities and terminology. They have mechanical timing and terminology checks; the five additional languages have not been independently reviewed by native speakers.

This repository contains the static teaching website and its actual media files, prepared for public GitHub Pages hosting at https://gateultra.github.io/placement-lesson6/. It does not include analytics or external model calls during viewing. The original production workspace retains raw speech, separate music and picture masters; those large intermediates are outside this website repository.
